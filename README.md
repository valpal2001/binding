# Project 3: *Binding*

This is Valerie Hui's repo for Project 3: Binding.

Here is my linked chat with Claude: https://claude.ai/share/c388c3ba-9320-4350-b220-d756539ce966

Direction one: Message lost in transit
Using tan kraft paper, monoplex text, and red stamp, this direction is meant to mimic the feeling of seeing documantion in a mail folder. With globalizaiton as the tying theme, articles of different origins come with equal weight to the same page.

Direction two: Live between white lines
Using a background of gradient lines , it keeps the writing within accepted parameters. It echoes sentiments in article two, that this uniformity in design thinking is a byproduct of white priviledge. 

Direction three: Black out boxes
What messages gets lost in the vastness? Black out boxes with white and grey text asks who gets included when writing design history.
